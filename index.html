About Bangla Trade Ventures

We are a trusted export partner connecting global buyers with high-quality natural products from Bangladesh. From eco-friendly jute and farm-fresh agricultural goods to premium leather materials, we ensure every product meets international standards for quality, sustainability, and traceability. Our mission is simple: Empower local producers. Serve global markets. Deliver with integrity. Whether you're a retailer, manufacturer, or distributor, we make cross-border sourcing simple, transparent, and reliable.

From Local Roots to Global Reach

It all began in the villages of Bangladesh, where farmers, artisans, and tanners have crafted natural products for generations. But many lacked access to international markets. We founded OriginExport to bridge that gap — to bring the world the authentic quality of our jute, rice, spices, and leather, while ensuring fair returns for the communities who grow and make them. Today, we work directly with farmers, cooperatives, and certified tanneries to deliver export-grade products with full documentation, sustainability proof, and on-time delivery.
